What it does

Forecasts for travelers,
and anyone in between.

Both scales

°F and °C, everywhere

A dual arc gauge with today's high and low, and every forecast, detail and widget in both scales. Choose which one comes first.

Forecasts

Read it at a glance

Next-hour rain, the next 24 hours and a 10-day outlook with highs, lows and chance of rain, plus severe weather alerts where available.

Learn

Get fluent in the other scale

Slide the ruler to convert any temperature, see today's low, high and feels-like on one scale, and pick up quick maths to estimate in your head.

Details

Every reading, charted

Feels like, UV index, wind, humidity, visibility, pressure, precipitation and cloud cover. Tap any reading for hour-by-hour charts.

Cities

Compare your places

Every city sits on one shared temperature scale, so home and away line up at a glance. Your list follows you through iCloud.

Sun & moon

Daylight and moonlight

Sunrise, sunset and hours of daylight for each day, and a moon drawn to match its phase, with moonrise and moonset.

Widgets & Watch

On your wrist,
and your Home Screen

An Apple Watch app with complications, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and on iPhone Duo and iPad, your cities beside a full forecast.

Private by design

Your weather,
nobody else's.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • Your location never reaches us. If you allow it, your coordinates go only to Apple's weather and maps services to get the forecast.
  • Your cities, in your iCloud. They sync through your own iCloud account, and stay on your devices. Data Not Collected. Read the privacy policy.
