Weather in two scales

DuoWeather

Fahrenheit and Celsius, side by side.

Every temperature in °F and °C at once, so you never have to convert in your head. Hourly and 10-day forecasts, weather alerts, moon phases, widgets and an Apple Watch app. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

What it does

  • Both scales, everywhere. The gauge, forecasts and widgets all show °F and °C together. Choose which comes first.
  • Forecasts you can read fast. Next-hour rain, the next 24 hours and a 10-day outlook.
  • Slide to convert. A built-in ruler and quick maths help you learn the other scale.
  • Compare your cities. Every city sits on one shared temperature scale, and your list follows you through iCloud.
  • Sun and moon. Sunrise, sunset, daylight and a moon drawn to match each day's phase.
  • Weather alerts. Severe weather warnings where they're available.
  • Widgets and Apple Watch. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Watch app and complications.

Made for bigger screens

On iPhone Duo and iPad, DuoWeather opens up into a city list beside a full forecast.
