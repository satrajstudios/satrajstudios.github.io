DuoWeather

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

DuoWeather doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your location, your cities or anything else from the app.

Your location

If you allow it, DuoWeather uses your location while the app is open to show the weather where you are. Your coordinates go to Apple's weather and maps services to get the forecast and the name of the place. They aren't sent to the developer. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, and search for cities instead.

Weather and city search

Forecasts come from Apple Weather through WeatherKit. When you search for a city, the words you type go to Apple's maps service to find matching places. Apple's privacy policy covers those services.

Your saved cities

Your cities, their order, your °F or °C preference and recent forecasts are stored on your device, where the widgets can read them. If you use an Apple Watch, the iPhone app shares your cities and forecasts with it directly.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your saved cities sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account. Your current location is never uploaded to iCloud. The developer can't see this data.

What DuoWeather doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by the developer.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a city to remove it from your list and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

DuoWeather doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
