Need a hand or found a bug? Email satrajstudios@gmail.com and say which device and iOS version you're using. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

How do I add a city?

Open the city list and tap Search for a city. Type a name, pick a result, then tap Add.

How do I remove or reorder cities?

Swipe left on a city to delete it. Press and hold a saved city, then drag it to a new position. My Location always stays at the top.

Can I show °C first?

Yes. In the city list, tap the … button and choose °C first or °F first. Both scales always stay on screen.

Why doesn't it show my location?

Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › DuoWeather and choose While Using the App. You can also search for your city instead.

Why don't I see next-hour rain or weather alerts?

These come from Apple Weather and aren't offered in every region. DuoWeather shows them wherever they're available.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add a widget and choose DuoWeather.

My cities aren't syncing

Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud is turned on. New changes can take a little while to arrive.

The forecast looks out of date

Pull down on a forecast to refresh it, or tap the … button in the city list and choose Refresh All.
