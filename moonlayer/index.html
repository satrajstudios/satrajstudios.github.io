What it does

The right step,
at the right time.

Now

Morning and night, in order

Open the app and see exactly what goes on next, from cleanser to sunscreen. Tick each step off as you go.

Schedules

Every step on its own rhythm

Daily, on chosen weekdays, monthly or just once. An exfoliant on Tuesdays and Saturdays, a peel on the first Sunday of the month.

Clashes

Spot actives that clash

Moon Layer checks the year ahead and flags pairs that are better kept apart, like a retinoid with an exfoliating acid, or a morning with no sunscreen.

Scan

Scan it, see what's inside

Scan a barcode or search by name, or photograph the ingredient list and Moon Layer reads it on your device. A simple score sums up the ingredients.

History

See what you did

Every tick is kept, day by day, so you can look back over a week and see what you actually used, and when.

Reminders

A nudge at bedtime

Morning and night reminders that list tonight's products, scheduled right on your device.

On iPad

Tonight, and the
week at a glance.

On iPad, tonight's steps sit beside a week-at-a-glance grid of every product, and the monthly and one-off steps coming up next.

Getting a new phone? Export a backup to Files or iCloud Drive and restore it there. Your routine, history and photos come with it.

Private by design

Your routine
stays on your shelf.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • On your device. Your routine, photos and history are saved on your iPhone or iPad. Backups go only where you choose to save them.
  • Lookups send only the product. A scan sends just the barcode, or the words you searched, to public product databases. Read the privacy policy.
