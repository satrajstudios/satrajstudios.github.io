Your morning and night routine, in the right order. Scan products, catch actives that clash, and tick off each step. No account, and everything stays on your device. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

  • Morning and night, in order. See exactly what to put on now, and tick each step off.
  • Every step on its own schedule. Daily, chosen weekdays, monthly, or just once.
  • Spot actives that clash. Retinoids with acids, vitamin C with benzoyl peroxide, and more.
  • Scan it, see what's inside. Barcode lookups with a simple ingredient score.
  • History and reminders. See what you did, and get a nudge that lists tonight's products.
