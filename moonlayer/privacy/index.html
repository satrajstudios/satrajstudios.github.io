Moon Layer

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Moon Layer doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your routine, photos and history stay on your device, and the developer can't see them.

Your routine and history

Moon Layer saves your steps, their schedules, the steps you tick off, your settings and your skin quiz answers (if you take it) in a file in the app's own folder on your device. Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer.

Camera and photos

Moon Layer uses the camera only when you choose to scan a barcode or photograph a product or its ingredient list. You can also pick product photos from your library. Photos you keep are scaled down and saved in the app's folder on your device. Reading an ingredient list from a photo happens on your device with Apple's text recognition.

Product lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, Moon Layer sends the barcode number or the words you searched to Open Beauty Facts, Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb to look the product up, and loads product photos from Open Beauty Facts. When it suggests alternatives, it sends only the product type (for example "cleanser"). No account, name, location or device identifier is sent with these requests. Those services' own privacy policies cover their servers.

Reminders

Morning and bedtime reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Backups

If you export a backup, Moon Layer creates a file with your routine, history and photos and saves it where you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Restoring reads a backup file you pick. The developer never receives these files.

What Moon Layer doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

In Moon Layer, Settings › Start over deletes your routine, history and photos. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Not medical advice

Ingredient checks and scores are general guidance, not medical advice. Moon Layer is rated 13+.
