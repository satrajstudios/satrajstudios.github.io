SFSD School App

Delete your data

Last updated 18 October 2026

To have your SFSD School App account and records deleted, email the Sikh Foundation San Diego Punjabi School. The school looks after the records behind the app, so it handles every deletion request. The app is made by Satraj Studios for the school.

How to ask

  1. Email PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org with the subject “SFSD School App data deletion request”. Please write from the email address on the account, so the school knows it's you.
  2. Tell the school the parent's name and your children's names.
  3. Say whether you'd like the whole family account deleted, or only specific records.

What gets deleted

The school will delete the account and the records it holds for it, such as homework submissions, attendance, grades and messages. In some cases the school may need to keep certain records because the law requires it. If so, it will tell you which records and for how long.

Data on your device

Signing out of the app removes the sign-in token and saved screens from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device.

More help

You can read how the app handles information in the privacy policy, or visit support for questions about the app itself.
