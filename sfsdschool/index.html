What it does

Everything from class,
wherever you are.

Students

Homework, handed in

See what's due, then submit by photographing your pages or attaching a file. Grades and teacher feedback come back to the same place.

Families

Every child, one login

Families who share an email can switch between their children to check homework, progress and attendance.

Teachers

Set, mark, reply

Post homework for your grade, mark submissions with written notes on the page, and answer students' questions.

Attendance

Registers & check-in

Take the register on a phone or tablet, or run the check-in station that scans students' QR badges at the door.

School news

Announcements & messages

School announcements on the home screen, holidays ahead, and messages between families and staff.

Anywhere

Phone, tablet, offline

Layouts made for iPhone, iPad and Android tablets. Screens you've opened recently still open with no connection.

Private by design

The school's data
stays with the school.

  • Talks only to the school's server. Nothing you see or send goes to anyone else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app has none built in.
  • Signed out means cleared. Signing out removes your sign-in and saved screens from the device. Read the privacy policy.
