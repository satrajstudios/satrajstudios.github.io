SFSD School App

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The SFSD School App is a window onto the Sikh Foundation San Diego Punjabi School's own system. Everything you see or send in the app goes between your device and the school's server at school.sdsikhs.org, and nowhere else. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who looks after your information

The school decides what it records about students, families and staff, and keeps those records on its server. The app is made by Satraj Studios for the school. To see, correct or delete school records, contact the school at PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org. For questions about the app itself, email satrajstudios@gmail.com.

Signing in

Parents and staff sign in with the email and password the school gave them. They are sent over an encrypted connection to the school's server, which sends back a sign-in token. The app never stores your password.

  • The sign-in token, and your name and role (so the app opens to the right screens), are kept in your device's secure storage: the Keychain on iPhone and iPad, and Android Keystore-protected storage on Android. They stay on that device and are never backed up.
  • Signing out deletes them from the device.

What the app shows and sends

Depending on whether you're a family member, teacher or admin, the app shows and sends information that already lives on the school's server, such as:

  • homework, the work you submit (including photos, scans and files), grades and teacher feedback;
  • attendance, terms, holidays and announcements;
  • messages and questions between families, students and staff;
  • for staff, student details and the QR badges used at the check-in station.

The app only sends something when you act on it, for example submitting homework, taking the register or sending a message.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use the camera to scan student QR badges at the check-in station and to photograph homework pages. If you attach photos or files, only the ones you choose are read. Pictures are prepared on your device and uploaded to the school's server only when you submit them. You can turn camera access off at any time in your device's settings; you can still attach files.

On Android, QR scanning uses Google's ML Kit and page scanning uses the document scanner in Google Play services. Both process images on your phone. Google may receive anonymous diagnostics about how these components perform, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Saved on your device

  • Recent screens. So screens open quickly and still work without a connection, the app keeps a copy of what it last showed you, for up to 30 days. It is kept separately for each person who signs in, is stored in protected storage that isn't backed up, and is deleted when you sign out.
  • Preferences. The school's server address, your appearance and layout choices, and which announcements you've dismissed.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Security

The app only connects to the school's server over HTTPS, and refuses connections it can't verify.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or third-party crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of information. Nothing is sent to anyone but the school's server.

Children

Children, including those under 13, don't sign in to the app themselves. Parents and guardians sign in with the family account the school gives them, and can view their children's homework, attendance and grades. The school creates all accounts. Parents can ask the school to review or delete their child's records at PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org. To ask for your data to be deleted, see Delete your data.

Test versions

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play's testing programs, Apple or Google may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, under their own terms.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about the app or this policy? Email satrajstudios@gmail.com. Questions about school records? Email PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org.
