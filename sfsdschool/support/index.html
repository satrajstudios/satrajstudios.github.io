SFSD School App

Support

Homework, attendance and messages for the Sikh Foundation San Diego Punjabi School, on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Found a bug or something not working in the app? Email satrajstudios@gmail.com and say which device you're using and what you were doing. For your account, homework or attendance records, contact the school at PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org.

How do I sign in?

Use the same email or student ID and password you use on the school's website. Accounts are created by the school, so there's nothing to sign up for in the app.

I forgot my password

Ask the school office or your teacher to reset it, or email PunjabiSchool@sdsikhs.org.

We have more than one child at the school

If your family shares one email, the app asks which child you'd like to see when you sign in. You can switch between children at any time on the Account screen.

How do I hand in homework?

Open the homework, then photograph your pages with the camera or attach a photo or PDF, and submit. You'll see it marked as submitted, and your grade and feedback appear in the same place once your teacher has marked it.

The camera isn't working

The app needs camera access to photograph homework and to scan QR badges at the check-in station. On iPhone and iPad, turn it on in Settings › SFSD School App › Camera. On Android, go to Settings › Apps › SFSD School App › Permissions.

Does it work without internet?

Screens you've opened in the last 30 days still open without a connection, showing what they showed last time. Submitting work, taking the register and sending messages need a connection.

Privacy

The app talks only to the school's server and has no ads or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
