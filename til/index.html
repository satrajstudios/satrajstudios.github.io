What it does

Made for the wait,
and the day itself.

Calendar

Bring in your calendar

Choose events you already have and Til counts down to them. It only keeps the ones you pick, and never changes your calendar.

Your own

Or make your own

Name it, pick a date, set a time or make it all day, and file it as a birthday, holiday, trip or anniversary.

Widgets

Home & Lock Screen

Small, medium and large widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and a glowing dot-matrix day counter.

Live

Live countdown

When the day is close, follow it on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, down to the second.

Reminders

Gentle nudges

On the day, the day before, two days before or a week ahead. Turn on as many as you like.

Everywhere

Wrist, iPad & Duo

An Apple Watch app with complications, side-by-side views on iPad and iPhone Duo, and iCloud sync between your devices.

Private by design

  • On your devices and your iCloud. Sync uses your private iCloud, which only you can see.
