Til

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Til doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your countdowns stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud, and the developer can't see them.

Your calendar

Til asks for access to your calendar only so you can pick events to count down to. It reads your events on your device after you allow access, and it never changes or adds anything to your calendar. Only the events you choose to track are saved by Til. If you don't allow access, you can still add countdowns by hand, and you can turn access on or off at any time in Settings.

Your countdowns

For each countdown you track, Til saves its name, date and time, category or calendar name, color, reminders and when you started tracking it. This is stored on your device, in a space shared with Til's own widgets so they can show your countdowns. Your appearance setting (light, dark or system) is stored on your device too.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Til keeps your countdowns in sync across your iPhone and iPad using your private iCloud database. That data belongs to your Apple Account: the developer has no access to it and can't read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your countdowns simply stay on the device.

Apple Watch

If you have a paired Apple Watch with Til installed, your iPhone sends your countdowns directly to the watch so the watch app and complications can show them. They don't pass through any server run by the developer.

Reminders and live countdowns

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Live countdowns on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island are started on your device. Neither uses a server run by the developer.

What Til doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Removing a countdown in Til deletes it from your device and from your iCloud, so it disappears from your other devices too. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. You can also manage Til's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Til is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.
