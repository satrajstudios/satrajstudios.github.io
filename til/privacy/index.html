Til

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Til doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your countdowns stay on your devices, and the developer can't see them. On iPhone and iPad they also sync through your own private iCloud, and on Android they can be backed up to your own Google Account.

Your calendar

Til asks for access to your calendar only so you can pick events to count down to. It reads your events on your device after you allow access, and it never changes or adds anything to your calendar. Only the events you choose to track are saved by Til. If you don't allow access, you can still add countdowns by hand, and you can turn access on or off at any time in Settings (on Android, under Settings › Apps › Til › Permissions).

Your countdowns

For each countdown you track, Til saves its name, date and time, category or calendar name, color, reminders and when you started tracking it. This is stored on your device. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in a space shared with Til's own widgets so they can show your countdowns; on Android, see Til for Android below. Your appearance setting (light, dark or system) is stored on your device too.

iCloud sync

On iPhone and iPad, if you're signed in to iCloud, Til keeps your countdowns in sync across your iPhone and iPad using your private iCloud database. That data belongs to your Apple Account: the developer has no access to it and can't read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your countdowns simply stay on the device. Til for Android doesn't use iCloud.

Apple Watch

On iPhone, if you have a paired Apple Watch with Til installed, your iPhone sends your countdowns directly to the watch so the watch app and complications can show them. They don't pass through any server run by the developer.

Reminders and live countdowns

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. On Apple devices, live countdowns on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island are started on your device. Neither uses a server run by the developer.

Til for Android

Til for Android works the same way and collects no data. It doesn't request internet access, so it can't send anything off your phone. The rest of this policy applies to it too, except for the iCloud, Apple Watch and Dynamic Island parts, which are specific to Apple devices.

  • Your calendar. Til asks for permission to read your calendar only so you can pick events to count down to. It never changes or adds anything to your calendar, and the permission is optional. You can allow or remove access at any time in Android Settings, under Apps › Til › Permissions.
  • Your countdowns. Each countdown's name, date and time, category or calendar name, color, reminders and when you started tracking it are stored in Til's private storage on your phone. Til's home screen widgets read the same stored countdowns, on your phone, to draw themselves. Your appearance setting is stored there too.
  • Backup. If you use Android Backup, your countdowns and appearance setting may be backed up to your own Google Account and restored on a new phone. That copy belongs to your Google Account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; the developer can't see it. You can turn backup off or delete it in your phone's Google settings.
  • Reminders. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone, and Android asks your permission before Til can show them. If you allow "Alarms & reminders" for Til in Android Settings, reminders arrive on time; if you don't, they still arrive, up to about ten minutes late. Til re-schedules reminders after your phone restarts. No server is involved.
  • Sharing. If you tap Share on a countdown, the text you share (for example "Mum's birthday is in 12 days") goes only to the app you choose. Til doesn't send it anywhere itself.
  • Deleting your data. Removing a countdown in Til deletes it from your phone. Uninstalling Til removes everything it stored on the phone. If you use Android Backup, you can delete the backup copy in your phone's Google settings.
  • No internet, accounts, ads or analytics. Til for Android has no internet access, no accounts or sign-in, no ads and no analytics or crash reporting.

What Til doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

On iPhone and iPad, removing a countdown in Til deletes it from your device and from your iCloud, so it disappears from your other devices too. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. You can also manage Til's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings. On Android, see Til for Android above.

Children

Til is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.
