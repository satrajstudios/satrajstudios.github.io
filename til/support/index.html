Support

Count down to the days that matter, on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android.

Need a hand or found a bug? Email satrajstudios@gmail.com and say which device and iOS or Android version you're using. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

Do I have to give Til access to my calendar?

No. Calendar access only lets you pick existing events to count down to. Without it you can still add countdowns by hand. You can turn access on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars on iPhone, or Settings › Apps › Til › Permissions on Android.

How do I add a widget on iPhone?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Til. Widgets show the countdowns you're tracking in the app.

My countdowns aren't syncing between devices

This applies to iPhone and iPad. Til syncs through your private iCloud. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Til in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

Til isn't showing on my Apple Watch

There's no Apple Watch app on Android. On iPhone, the watch app comes with the iPhone app. Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Til under Available Apps and tap Install. Your countdowns are sent from your iPhone, so open Til on your iPhone once after installing.

I'm not getting reminders

On iPhone, check that notifications are allowed for Til in Settings › Notifications › Til, and that a Focus isn't silencing them. On Android, check Settings › Apps › Til › Notifications, and that Do Not Disturb isn't on.

How do I add a Til widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, and search for Til. Drag out the Til widget (a paper card that follows light and dark) or the Dot Matrix widget (a glowing night counter). Stretch either one wider to see a list of your next countdowns, and tap a widget to open that countdown.

How do I choose which countdown a widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget and tap its settings (on some phones this is a pencil or a Settings button), then pick a countdown. Until you choose one, a widget shows your next countdown.

Why are my Android reminders a few minutes late?

Android can delay reminders by up to about ten minutes unless Til is allowed to set exact alarms. To get them on time, open Settings › Apps › Til and allow Alarms & reminders. Also check that notifications are allowed for Til.

Do my countdowns move to a new Android phone?

Yes, if you use Android Backup. Your countdowns and appearance setting are backed up to your own Google Account and come back when you set up a new phone and restore from that backup.

Do countdowns sync between my iPhone and Android phone?

No. Til keeps your countdowns on each device separately, so an iPhone and an Android phone each have their own list.

How can I join the Android test?

Til for Android is in testing on Google Play and isn't public yet. To join, email satrajstudios@gmail.com with the Gmail address you use on your Android phone, and we'll send you an invite.
