Support

Count down to the days that matter, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Need a hand or found a bug? Email satrajstudios@gmail.com and say which device and iOS version you're using. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

Do I have to give Til access to my calendar?

No. Calendar access only lets you pick existing events to count down to. Without it you can still add countdowns by hand. You can turn access on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Til. Widgets show the countdowns you're tracking in the app.

My countdowns aren't syncing between devices

Til syncs through your private iCloud. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Til in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

Til isn't showing on my Apple Watch

The watch app comes with the iPhone app. Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Til under Available Apps and tap Install. Your countdowns are sent from your iPhone, so open Til on your iPhone once after installing.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Til in Settings › Notifications › Til, and that a Focus isn't silencing them.
